# Instrument

This folder contains questionnaires administered online during each wave, both in English and Spanish. 
The data based on some of the questions provided in these questionnaires is not shared due to two main reasons:
- the data is under embargo
- providing this data together with the main respondent information risks violating de-identifiability.

In the survey questionnaire, participants were asked to: 
- provide information about themselves (their child) if not gathered previousely
- contextualize the previous day (“yesterday”). Examples:
  - whether they were sick yesterday
  - whether they worked or attended school yesterday
  - their mode of attendance (in-person or remote).
- record the list of contacts they had yesterday (contact diary)
- provide information on each contact (e.g., age, race/ethnicity, sex) and their relationship (e.g., person they live with, classmate/teacher, relative, friend).

To facilitate respondents’ recall, the contact diary was divided into four sections based on the setting of the contact: home, school, work, or “other” (community). 
Participants were instructed to recall contacts they had yesterday from the time they woke up until the time they went to sleep.
They were also provided with examples of contact diaries for situations similar to theirs (e.g., a hypothetical student going to school, a hypothetical teacher/worker going to work).
