# Observatory of US Contact Patterns

[**Documentation**]() | [**Website**]() | [**Tutorials**](https://github.com/allisandrak/Observatory-of-US-Contact-Patterns/tree/main/tutorials) 

The **Observatory of US Contact Patterns** contains post-COVID-19 pandemic contact patterns data for a sample of the US that is nationally representative by age, sex, and race/ethnicity as well as the respective estimated contact matrices, allowing users to simulate the spread of respiratory infectious diseases through different population groups using current US social mixing patterns.
