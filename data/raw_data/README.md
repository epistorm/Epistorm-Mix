
This folder contains cleaned data before imputations and is provided here for reproducibility purposes and potential sensitivity analyses if an alternative missing strategy is considered. 
This data contains missing data. Contacts reported as a group are represented as a set of variables in respondent data here: "resp_edun_xx" for either multiple or numerous contacts with children/students of xx age group; mc_ncont for other numerous/multiple contacts. Such contacts were split into multiple contacts (<30 contacts reported as a group) and numerous contacts (>= 30 contacts reported as a group) in the analysis. As a result, socio-demographic characteristics of multiple contacts are imputed in the imputed data, while numerous contacts continue to be reported as a separate variable.

We encourage utilizing [imputed data](../imputed_data) for any mathematical modeling and further contact pattern analyses.
